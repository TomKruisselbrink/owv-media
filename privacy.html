Privacy Policy – ReelMaker Publisher

Last updated: 17 November 2026

ReelMaker Publisher is a private tool used only by the owner of the Instagram account @onboardwithverstappen and the Facebook page Onboardwithverstappen. It is used to publish the owner's own posts, reels and images to these accounts.

What data is collected

None. The app does not collect, store, sell or share personal data of any other users. It only accesses the owner's own Instagram account and Facebook page, through the official Meta Graph API, in order to publish content and check whether it was published.

Third parties

Images are temporarily hosted on GitHub so Instagram can fetch them while a post is being published. They contain only the owner's own content and no personal data of others.

Data deletion

Because the app stores no data about other users, there is nothing to delete. If you have interacted with the Facebook page or Instagram account and want to request deletion of any data, send a message to the Facebook page Onboardwithverstappen or a direct message to @onboardwithverstappen on Instagram. Requests are handled within 30 days.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent via a message to the Facebook page Onboardwithverstappen or to @onboardwithverstappen on Instagram.
